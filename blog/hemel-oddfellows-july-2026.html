18 July 2026 Gig Recap

Cover Loverz light up The Oddfellows Arms

On Friday 17 July we returned to one of our favourite venues, The Oddfellows Arms in Hemel Hempstead, and what a night it turned out to be. The set covered all kinds — rock, pop, soul and plenty in between — and the crowd sang along from the first chorus to the final encore. The night was made even more special by a few guest appearances on stage, adding some brilliant one-off moments you only get with live music.

Huge thanks to the Oddfellows team and everyone who came down. We love playing there, and we're delighted to say we'll be back in 2027. Keep an eye on our gig listings and we'll see you down the front.

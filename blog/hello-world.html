12 July 2026 News

Welcome to the Cover Loverz blog

Hello and welcome to our brand new blog! This is where we'll be sharing gig recaps, band news, behind-the-scenes bits, and anything else worth shouting about.

If you've caught us live before, you know what we're about — four of us, a whole lot of songs you already know the words to, and a mission to give you the best night out you've had in a while. If you haven't seen us yet — well, keep reading.

Coming up this Friday

We're back at The Oddfellows Arms in Hemel Hempstead on Friday 17th July. Doors are open, entry is free, and we'll be on stage from around 9pm to 11:30pm.

The Oddfellows has become one of our favourite local rooms — the crowd always brings the energy, the bar staff are great, and it's the perfect kick-off to the weekend. Bring your friends, bring your dancing shoes, bring requests. We'll do our best to bury the last work email of the week under a wall of guitars.

Full details are on our Gigs page, and you can see everything upcoming on our Lemonrock listing.

What's next

We'll be back here with a proper recap after Friday — photos, setlist highlights, and probably a story or two we shouldn't be telling. Longer term, expect posts on new songs we're learning, kit updates, and shout-outs to the venues that keep having us back.

See you Friday.

— The Cover Loverz
